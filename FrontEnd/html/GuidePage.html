THE ARCHER'S CHRONICLE

GUIDE

Learn the ways of the bow.

01. AIM

Hold your mouse and drag away from your archer to aim your bow.

The farther you pull, the stronger your shot becomes.

AIMING
ARROW

02. SHOOT

Release the mouse button to fire your arrow.

Your arrow follows a curved path through the air.

03. HEADSHOTS

Enemies have different collision zones.

Strike the head to deal critical damage.

HEADSHOT
APPLE

04. APPLES

Apples appear in the battlefield.

Hit an apple with your arrow to restore some health.

05. ENEMY ARROWS

Your enemy will fire arrows toward your position.

Some arrows will miss. Some will hit.

ENEMY
WAVES

06. WAVES

Defeat your enemy and advance to the next wave.

Each wave becomes more difficult.
