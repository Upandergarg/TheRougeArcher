How To Be

Rogue Archer

Learn the ways of the bow.

01

Aim Your Shot

Move your mouse around the battlefield to aim your bow. Your character will automatically turn the bow toward your cursor.

02

Hold & Release

Hold the left mouse button to prepare your shot. Release the mouse button to fire the arrow toward your current aim.

03

Master The Trajectory

Arrows travel forward and are affected by gravity. This makes their path curve downward, so aim above your target when necessary.

04

Headshots Deal More Damage

Aim carefully at the enemy's head for a devastating headshot. A headshot instantly defeats the enemy. Body hits deal 25 damage.

05

Survive The Counterattack

The enemy will fire arrows back at you. A head hit deals 25 damage while a body hit deals 10 damage. Watch your health and avoid incoming arrows.

06

Earn Coins & Find Pickups

Defeating an enemy gives you 100 score and 10 coins. Health pickups restore your health, while arrow pickups give you 20 additional arrows. Keep fighting and build your highest score.
